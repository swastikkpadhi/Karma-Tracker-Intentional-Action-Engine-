Core UI & UX Architecture
The Karmic Wheel (Primary Dashboard): Instead of standard progress bars, habits populate an interactive Chakra / Wheel of Action. Completing daily actions expands the geometric rings of the wheel.

Guna Classification System: Users tag every daily action or habit with its underlying energy quality:

Sattvic (Purity & Harmony): Actions driven by wisdom, health, service, and self-care (e.g., meditation, studying, healthy eating, helping someone).

Rajasic (Passion & Drive): Goal-oriented, high-output actions (e.g., deep work sessions, workout routines, financial planning, pitch decks).

Tamasic (Inertia & Distraction): Habits to minimize (e.g., doomscrolling, procrastination, emotional reactivity).

Key Features
Karma Breakdown (Sankalpa-to-Karma Mapping):

Every major goal (Sankalpa) breaks down into actionable micro-tasks (Karmas).

Example: If the Sankalpa is "Build financial independence (Artha)," the micro-Karmas are "Review monthly budget (Rajasic)" and "Spend 30 mins learning market trends (Sattvic)."

Nishkama Karma Toggle (Action Without Attachment):

Inspired by the Bhagavad Gita ("You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions").

A daily reflection prompt that encourages users to check off tasks while logging their emotional detachment from the outcome—focusing purely on execution and mastery.

Karmic Balance Score:

An algorithmic score evaluating the balance between Rajasic drive (ambition) and Sattvic alignment (inner peace), warning users if they are leaning into burnout or procrastination.
